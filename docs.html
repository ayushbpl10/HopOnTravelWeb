Fast Track

⚡ 3-Minute Quick Start Guide

Follow these 4 essential steps to get your first adventure live and open for bookings in minutes:

  • 1

    Open the Vendor Hub & Sign In

    Navigate to abtohghoomle.com/vendor-portal.html and click "Vendor Sign in with Google". You can also test all features instantly using the demo account button.

  • 2

    Create Your First Listing

    Click "✨ AI WhatsApp Import" to paste your group chat itinerary or choose "⚡ Use Template" to load presets like *Harishchandragad* or *Rajmachi* in 1 click.

  • 3

    Share Your Branded Storefront Link

    Copy your unique link (abtohghoomle.com/vendor.html?id=YOUR_ID) and paste it into your Instagram bio, WhatsApp status, and link-in-bio trees.

  • 4

    Manage Bookings & Broadcast Live GPS

    Confirm seat payments, download Excel passenger lists with 1 click, and broadcast your bus location so travellers can track you live.

💡
0% Commission Policy: Ab Toh Ghoom Le never takes a percentage cut of your trip revenue. All booking amounts go directly to your personal UPI ID / bank account.

AI Automation

✨ AI WhatsApp Itinerary Importer

Organisers usually spend 20 minutes copying trip details from WhatsApp into website forms. Our parser reduces this to 5 seconds:

📋
How to use: Open your WhatsApp group, copy the trip message, open "✨ AI WhatsApp Import" in the dashboard, paste the message, and click "Parse with AI".

Example WhatsApp message that works instantly:

*Rajmachi Fireflies Night Trek & Camping* Dates: 12-13 October 2026 Cost: Rs 1399/- per head Pickup Points: - Dadar TT Circle: 10:00 PM - Thane Teen Hath Naka: 11:15 PM Includes: Transport from Mumbai by private bus, Dinner, Breakfast, Tent stay, Guide. Excludes: Personal expenses. Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

The parser automatically extracts the title, price (1399), category (Trekking), batch dates, stops, and inclusions!

Inventory Control

📅 Batches & Seat Capacities

Manage multiple departure weekends without duplicating trip listings:

  • Multi-Batch Dates: Add upcoming departures (e.g. Batch 1: Oct 12-13, Batch 2: Oct 19-20).
  • Seat Capacities: Set bus seats (e.g., 30 seats). As tickets are confirmed, seats decrease automatically.
  • Automated Sold Out: When 30/30 seats are booked, the batch automatically closes to prevent overbooking.

Assistance

❓ Organiser FAQ & Support

Q: Does Ab Toh Ghoom Le charge commission per booking?

A: No. Zero commission. Travellers pay directly to your UPI ID or bank account.

Q: Can I edit an existing trip after publishing?

A: Yes! Go to your Vendor Dashboard, find the trip card, and click "Edit" to modify dates, price, or pickup points anytime.

Q: How do travellers receive their tickets?

A: Travellers receive an instant digital booking confirmation card with their Booking ID (e.g. ATGL-49074) and can look up their live ticket on traveller.html anytime.
